# Code of conduct

Veto is built by people with different backgrounds, levels of experience
and opinions. We want working on it to be a good experience for all of
them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be specific and constructive in reviews. Explain the reason behind a
  request, and accept that there is often more than one reasonable answer.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was new once, and a clear answer to
  a basic question helps everyone who reads it later.
- Credit others' work, and own your mistakes.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, including personal attacks, insults, slurs,
  sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual orientation.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, and other conduct that would be
  inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
channel where someone represents the project, including events.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-repository-responsibly/managing-disruptive-comments)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially,
and nobody is penalised for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide how to respond to reports, in proportion to what
happened: a private conversation, a public or private warning, a temporary
ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow
this code in good faith may be removed from their role.
